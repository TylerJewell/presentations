Principle 05

Controls come from three sources.

Regulatory, corporate, or project-scoped — enforced identically either way.

Regulatory & legislative
Law, regulator guidance, and industry standards already in the corpus.

Corporate policy
Inherited from a rule the enterprise already has written down.
“No agent approves a refund over $500 without review.”
Project-scoped
Written by the project team for a risk unique to this system.
“This underwriting agent may never see an applicant's SSN.”
