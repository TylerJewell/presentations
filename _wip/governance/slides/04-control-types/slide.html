Principle 04

Six building blocks, one job each.

Combine them to construct and enforce any part of the posture.

GuardrailBlocks or passes an action at a runtime boundary. E.g. stop a wire transfer lacking dual sign-off.
EvaluationMeasures and scores behavior, live or in test. E.g. score responses weekly for parity drift.
HumanA named person holds authority over the decision. E.g. a loan officer approves adverse-action notices.
GateTurns a check into a build or release decision. E.g. block deploys lacking a bias-audit attestation.
SanitizerStrips or masks content before it moves on. E.g. redact an applicant's SSN before the model sees it.
PolicyAn authored rule set that selects which controls apply. E.g. EU data auto-inherits the EU AI Act set.
