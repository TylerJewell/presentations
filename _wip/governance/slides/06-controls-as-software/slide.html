Principle 06

Controls are managed like software.

Bundled, signed, and enforced continuously — until the next version replaces it.

Bundle.
Chosen controls become one versioned package, pinned to a corpus snapshot.
Sign.
A named, accountable owner attests this bundle is what the system runs under.
Enforce.
Every control in it runs on every request — not sampled, not scheduled.
Release.
A new bundle supersedes it; the old one stays signed, pinned, and provable.
