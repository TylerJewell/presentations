Principle 07 · Enforce your posture

Compliance is proven across every environment.

The same controls execute in documents, pre-production, and production — Akka aggregates all three into one view.

Documents
Checks whether a required attestation is on file and still fresh — not runtime behavior.
Pre-production
A new Eval Matrix is built and certified here — evals, red teaming — against production-mirrored traffic.
Production
The certified Eval Matrix runs continuously, enforcing every control on live traffic.
Only a certified Eval Matrix may move to production.
