Principle 09 · Enforce your posture

Governance should apply everywhere.

Route any agent's traffic through Akka — legacy, third-party, or your own — and the same controls enforce, no matter who built it.

Built on AkkaControls run natively in the runtime — no separate step required.
Everything elseRoute its model and tool calls through Akka's AI gateway; most controls still apply.
Live, not observedIt blocks, sanitizes, and authorizes in the request path, not after the fact.
