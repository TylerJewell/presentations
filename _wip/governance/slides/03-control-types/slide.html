Principle 03

Every posture reduces to five controls.

A control is a discrete, provable check; combine the five to enforce any part of the posture.

GuardrailBlocks or passes an action at a runtime boundary.Stop a wire transfer lacking dual sign-off.
EvaluationMeasures and scores behavior, live or in test.Score responses weekly for parity drift.
HumanA named person holds authority over the decision.A loan officer approves adverse-action notices.
GateTurns a check into a build or release decision.Block deploys lacking a bias-audit attestation.
SanitizerStrips or masks content before it moves on.Redact an applicant's SSN before the model sees it.
