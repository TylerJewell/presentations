Principle 02

Postures have a lifecycle.

Independent of the AI system itself — and it's always evolving.

Intent
  • What is its purpose?
  • Who is accountable?
  • Where does it operate?
Autonomy
  • What decisions can it influence?
  • How autonomous may it be?
  • When must humans intervene?
Boundaries
  • Who can be affected?
  • What data may it use?
  • What dependencies shape it?
Evidence
  • What regulations govern it?
  • What evidence must it produce?
  • Who attests to it?
