Principle 10

Observability turns spend into a feedback loop.

Every call is measured, by team and by system — so cost and performance get tuned continuously, not audited after the fact.

Observe
Every model call is measured — by team, use case, and system.
Scrutinize
Each team sees its own system's spend and gets feedback for tuning it.
Compare
Continuously test and compare models on cost and performance, not just at launch.
