Principle 10

The AI gateway makes every token observable.

Not estimated — measured. Used to govern access, and to make the system more efficient.

Govern
See and control which agents call which models, by department and use case.
Optimize
Spot wasteful calls, cheaper routing, and caching opportunities automatically.
Iterate
Optimize output feeds back into the lifecycle, same as a compliance finding.
