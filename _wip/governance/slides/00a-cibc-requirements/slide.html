CIBC Requirements

Govern AI agents built outside their platform (e.g., Copilot, Appian)

Show how governance controls can be applied to externally built applications.

Enforce our policies in real time

Show how internal policies can be translated into machine-enforceable controls.

Demonstrate a policy violation and the resulting response.

Continuous guardrails and drift management

Show how an AI application remains compliant over time.

Examples: runtime guardrails, prompt changes, newly connected tools, risk re-assessment triggers, etc.

Financial governance (FinOps)

Show AI spend management at the enterprise, department, use case, application, and owner levels.

Examples: alerts, thresholds, hard caps, chargeback/show lineage, budget enforcement.

Discovery, intake, and lifecycle governance

Show how a new AI use case moves from idea → intake → risk assessment → approval → deployment → monitoring → change management → retirement.

Demonstrate how workflows, questionnaires, risk ratings, gating, and audit trails can be customized to align with our process.
