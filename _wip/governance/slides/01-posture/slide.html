Principle 01

Your posture already exists. Uniform enforcement doesn't.

Risk and compliance posture is set at the business level — applying it uniformly, everywhere, is the hard part.

Today
Each team builds first, then bolts on observation to see if the posture held.
Akka
That same posture becomes the specification — how the system is built and run.
Result
One posture governs implementation and operation, not one per project.
