Principle 01

Controls are defined once. Posture is proved everywhere.

An AI system proves it does what it was authorized to do — risk appetite, regulatory duties, and policy, translated into controls and enforced consistently.

Decide
Each business sets its own risk appetite, regulatory duties, and policy.
Define
Those decisions become controls, specified independent of any one AI system.
Enforce
Controls are enforced consistently, producing a posture you can prove.
