Principle 08

Environments keep the lifecycle continuous.

A new bundle is tested and certified before it ever reaches production.

Pre-production
A new bundle is built and certified here — evals, red teaming — against production-mirrored traffic.
Documents
Checks whether a required attestation is on file and still fresh — not runtime behavior.
Production
The certified bundle runs continuously, enforcing every control on live traffic.
Nothing reaches production without being proven here first.
