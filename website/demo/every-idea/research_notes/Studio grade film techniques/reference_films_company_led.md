# Company-led 2025-2026 brand films: catalogue

Scope: one flagship 2025 or 2026 film per company, in the priority order given. Sources are written descriptions only. No film was watched. Entries marked "snippet" come from search-result text and were not confirmed on a fetched page. Where a heading has no source, the entry says "not found" and nothing is inferred.

Coverage: 8 companies have an entry with at least one fetched or snippet source (Vercel, Notion, Figma, Anthropic, OpenAI, Apple, Google, Ramp). Linear, Stripe and Cursor: no 2025 or 2026 flagship film found. Microsoft: a Copilot spot is found, but its year is unconfirmed, so it is listed as unverified.

## 3. Vercel

- **Film:** "Behind the Ship" is the title of a post about an original animated short that opened Vercel Ship. The film itself has no title in the post.
- **Date:** post dated June 4, 2025. The film's own release date is not stated.
- **URL:** https://basement.studio/post/behind-the-ship (fetched)
- **Length:** not stated.

- **Typography:** not found.
- **Motion and 3D (maker's own statements):**
  - "We leaned into a technique known as animating on twos, holding each pose for two frames."
  - "We animated using master keys: the character's core movements and defining poses."
  - "Every animation in this project was done entirely by hand, pose by pose."
  - "Built with a node network of position-based masks, procedural noise, and layered shaders."
  - Built entirely in Blender, with a custom material developed in Blender. Render engines: EEVEE and Cycles.
- **Live action or photography:** none. The film is fully CG and animated.
- **Narration:** not found.
- **Tools and pipeline named by the maker:** Blender, ZBrush, Substance Painter, Adobe Photoshop, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve (grade and Fusion). Preview prerenders, EXR passes, Cryptomatte. Concept art and stills matched to each scene (tool not named).

## Patterns across the entries

Count = number of entries (out of 8 sourced entries: Vercel, Notion, Figma, Anthropic, OpenAI, Apple, Google, Ramp) whose written sources state the pattern. "Not stated" means the source was silent. Silence is not evidence that the pattern is absent.

| Pattern | Entries | Which |
|---|---|---|
| Written source names a credited outside director or production company | 4 of 8 | Anthropic (Mother, McAuliffe), OpenAI (Alto, SMUGGLER, Molloy), Apple (Jonze), Ramp (Arts & Letters, ProdCo). Notion names an agency but no director. |
| Written source names an in-house or agency team instead of a credited director | 3 of 8 | Vercel (Basement Studio), Notion (Instrument in-house editors and motion designers), Google (Google In-House) |
| Product UI shown as real screens | 0 of 8 | Figma article says the UI is not shown; Notion uses abstracted UI; Anthropic, OpenAI, Ramp, Apple, Google, Vercel: not stated |
| Product UI redrawn or abstracted | 1 of 8 | Notion ("abstracted UI ingredients"). Figma uses graphics, not UI. |
| Gesture-driven motion (graphics choreographed to input) | 1 of 8 | Figma (graphics react to the clicking hand) |
| Macro or close-up of hands or input devices | 1 of 8 | Figma (close-ups of mouse, trackpad, keyboard) |
| Hand-made 2D-on-twos or pose-by-pose animation | 1 of 8 | Vercel |
| CG rendered in Blender | 1 of 8 | Vercel |
| Practical sets, gimbals or in-camera effects | 2 of 8 | OpenAI (custom gimbal sets), Figma dance film (in-camera, but undated and not one of the 8 entries) |
| Live action or real-world photography | 5 of 8 | Anthropic (montage), OpenAI (live action with VFX), Apple (live action), Ramp (finance settings), Figma (hand photography) |
| Narration stated as absent | 0 of 8 | No source states "no narration." Anthropic has an unattributed voice. |
| Narration or voice stated as present | 1 of 8 | Anthropic (an unattributed voice asks a question; role unclear) |
| Opens on a question or statement in voice | 1 of 8 | Anthropic |
| Opens on a story premise | 1 of 8 | Vercel (vibe coder in NYC) |
| Music or sound credited by name | 0 of 8 | Notion lists sound design and music production roles with no tracks named. Figma dance film names a track but is undated. |
| Typography described in writing | 1 of 8 | Figma (tagline; no typeface named). Vercel's post does not describe typography for the film. |
| Named pipeline or tools | 2 of 8 | Vercel (Blender, ZBrush, Substance, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, EXR, Cryptomatte); Google (Gemini AI scene generation, but in a customer story, not the Google film). Anthropic, OpenAI, Apple, Ramp, Notion, Figma: not named. |

Note on the table: a pattern present in written sources but not stated for every film is recorded as "not stated," not as absent. The counts above are the sources that name the pattern, out of 8.
