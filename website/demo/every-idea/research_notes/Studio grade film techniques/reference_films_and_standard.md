# Studio-grade B2B technology films, product launch films and keynotes (as of 2026-11-18)

Scope note: this pass used 13 tool calls. Search results were mostly vendor listicles and agency blogs. Primary studio pages and credits were reachable for only a few films. The catalogue below is thin. It does not meet the 12 to 20 film target with full technique and opening-10-second breakdowns, and the gaps are listed under each key question. Nothing below was watched; technique claims come from studio or press text, not from viewing the films.

Date convention: "undated" means the fetched page gave no release date.

## Motion, typography and transition trends named for 2026

### Takeaway
Vendor and agency trend pages agree on kinetic typography and on 3D as a durable trend. They disagree on whether glossy polish or grit is rising. No independent jury or annual report was found.

### Cited Findings
- Kinetic typography is cited as the most-named trend, partly because short-form video is watched muted. Source: [Tangence, 2026 motion trends](https://www.tangence.com/blog/?p=1093) and [Advids trend round-up](https://advids.co/blog/ui-animation-motion-design-trends). Vendor blogs; unverified.
- 3D is described as "one of the most durable" design trends; hybrid 2D and 3D is "one of the strongest trends." Source: [Designity, 2026 2D animation trends](https://designity.com/blog/2d-animation-trends-2026) and the [Tangence](https://www.tangence.com/blog/?p=1093) roundup. Vendor blogs; unverified.
- One 2026 piece says over-polished, glossy production is losing ground, and rewards "grit, grain, paper edges, and jump cuts." Source: a [graphicdesignjunction trends piece, 2026-01](https://graphicdesignjunction.com/2026/01/video-and-motion-creative-trends-2026/). Commentary; unverified.
- Motion is framed as core brand identity, not an afterthought. Source: [Renderforest, Design Trends 2026](https://www.renderforest.com/blog/design-trends-in-2026). Vendor blog; unverified.
- Tooling news: Maxon made Autograph free for individual artists in April 2026 and later for studios on a Teams license. Source: [CGChannel, 2026-04-15](https://www.cgchannel.com/?p=175392) and [Maxon, What's new](https://www.maxon.net/en/maxon-one/new). Tool news, not a trend finding.

### Inferences
- The most consistent 2026 signal across sources is typography carrying the film, with 3D and grain as counterweights. This is a secondary-source consensus, not a jury finding.

### Gaps
- No Motion Awards 2026, Annecy 2026, D&AD 2026 or Brand New 2026 trend data was found. The named "2026 trends" in the brief are not confirmed by an authoritative source.

## Visual and audio tells of AI-generated or low-effort video, and what studios do instead

### Takeaway
The searches found little practitioner criticism. Most sources are vendors of AI video tools, who write that AI needs planning and direction. Two claims are specific enough to cite: AI faces break down when a character must perform, and custom characters and complex narrative sequences are beyond current AI motion tools. Both are vendor or agency claims.

### Cited Findings
- AI-generated video "can easily feel inconsistent, disconnected, or even unnatural" without clear planning and storyboarding. Source: [Motion The Agency, 2026-09](https://www.motiontheagency.com/blog/ai-assisted-video-production). Agency blog; the agency sells human-directed work.
- Custom illustrated characters, mascots and complex narrative sequences are "beyond what current AI motion graphics tools handle well." Source: [Malloy, 2026-04](https://www.malloy.sg/blog/ai-motion-designer-do-you-still-need-one-in-2026). Agency blog; unverified.
- "AI-generated faces break down the moment a character has to perform." Source: [invideo, 2026-08](https://invideo.io/faq/what-are-the-biggest-limitations-of-ai-video-generation/). Vendor FAQ; unverified.
- An AI video vendor advises designers to avoid complex physical actions that distort the subject's geometry. Source: [Dreamina, 2026-06](https://dreamina.capcut.com/ai-video/how-designerd-use-ai-video-tools-to-animate-campaign-visuals). Vendor guide; unverified.
- Counter-trend: grit, grain and jump cuts (see the motion section above).

### Inferences
- Tells that the sources support, by implication: inconsistent characters across shots, faces that fail in performance, geometry that shifts during motion, and smooth over-polish. None of these is measured.
- "What studios do instead" is supported only in general terms here: storyboard first, human direction, and real product captures. The specific studio practices (shot lists, reference-to-frame control, sound design by a named engineer) were not documented by any source found.

### Gaps
- Practitioner forum and trade-press criticism of AI-made films (designers' own posts on X, Reddit, Motionographer) was not found. Audio tells (voice-clone artifacts, music-bed loops, room tone) were not sourced at all.
