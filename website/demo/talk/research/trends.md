## 1. EU AI Act

**1.1 Phased timeline as enacted in 2024.**
- Claim: The Act entered into force 1 August 2024; prohibitions and AI literacy applied 2 February 2025; GPAI model rules and governance applied 2 August 2025; the remainder applies 2 August 2026 unless specified otherwise.
- Source figure: "1 August: The Act enters into force." "2 February [2025]: Prohibitions on certain AI systems and AI literacy requirements begin to apply." "2 August [2025]: Rules for GPAI models, governance, notified bodies, confidentiality, and certain penalties begin to apply."
- Source: Implementation timeline, artificialintelligenceact.eu (Future of Life Institute), last updated 31 Aug 2026. https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/implementation-timeline/
- Confidence: Secondary (tracker; consistent with the CSA note and Hogan Lovells via JD Supra). I did not open the text of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689.

**1.2 Digital Omnibus on AI: high-risk delay, now law.**
- Claim: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, adopted 8 July 2026 and published in the Official Journal 24 July 2026, moves Annex III high-risk obligations to 2 December 2027 and Annex I to 2 August 2028.
- Source figure (timeline page): "2 December [2027]: Requirements for high-risk systems under Annex III begin to apply." "2 August [2028]: Requirements for high-risk systems under Annex I begin to apply." Previous dates: Annex III 2 Aug 2026; Annex I 2 Aug 2027 (Hogan Lovells via JD Supra, 8 May 2026).
- Legislative steps (CSA note, 1 Aug 2026): Commission proposal 19 Nov 2025; provisional agreement 7 May 2026; Parliament plenary 16 Jun 2026 (423 for, 57 against, 174 abstentions); Council approval 29 Jun 2026; OJ 24 Jul 2026; in force 27 Jul 2026 (third day after publication).
- Title, confirmed on the Northern Ireland Assembly EU-law scrutiny tracker: "Regulation (EU) 2026/1744 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 8 July 2026 amending Regulations (EU) 2024/1689, (EU) 2018/1139 and (EU) 2023/1230 ... (Digital Omnibus on AI)"; OJ publication 24/07/2026.
- Sources: https://labs.cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/csa-research-note-eu-ai-act-high-risk-deadline-omnibus-20260/ ; https://www.niassembly.gov.uk/assembly-business/brexit-and-beyond/eu-law-scrutiny-tracker/regulation-2026-1744/ ; https://www.jdsupra.com/legalnews/eu-legislators-agree-to-delay-for-high-5617765/ ; https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/implementation-timeline/
- Confidence: Title, number and OJ date from a government-body tracker (close to primary). Entry-into-force date and vote counts from one source (CSA), with law-firm headlines in search results saying the Omnibus "enters into force". I could not open EUR-Lex. Verify there before quoting 27 July or the vote tally.

**1.3 Dates that did not move.**
- Claim: Article 50 transparency duties applied from 2 August 2026; the Article 50(2) watermarking duty for generative systems already on the market is 2 December 2026; new prohibitions on non-consensual intimate imagery and CSAM apply 2 December 2026; GPAI models placed on the market before 2 Aug 2025 must comply by 2 August 2027.
- Source figure: "2 December [2026]: Prohibitions on AI systems generating non-consensual intimate imagery and child sexual abuse material apply. Providers of pre-existing generative AI systems must also comply with Article 50(2) by this date." (artificialintelligenceact.eu). CSA note: Article 50 duties "applied from 2 August 2026".
- Confidence: Secondary; the timeline page and CSA agree.

**1.4 Cadence point.** The original high-risk date (2 Aug 2026) to the new date (2 Dec 2027) is a 16-month slip, agreed politically on 7 May 2026, under three months before the original date. Arithmetic mine, from the dates above.

## 7. The gap

**7.1 Guidance versus agents.** US bank model-risk guidance rewritten in April 2026 expressly excludes generative and agentic AI (5.2), and its validation language is that "timing, nature, and frequency ... vary", with no fixed interval. OSFI E-23 likewise sets no fixed interval (3.1). Both primary. Neither prescribes continuous review.

**7.2 Governance versus agents (IBM/Censuswide, Sept 2026).** 37% say governance is designed to scale with agentic AI; 80% say an AI-related incident reached their board or C-suite in the past year, but only 40% of those organisations changed AI governance policy or frameworks in response; 98% have assigned board-level responsibility for AI risk; 26% have a dedicated board-level AI committee. Secondary (news report of vendor-commissioned survey, EMEA only).

## Do not claim

- "The EU AI Act high-risk rules apply from 2 August 2026." Outdated; moved to 2 Dec 2027 (Annex III) and 2 Aug 2028 (Annex I). Article 50 transparency duties did apply 2 Aug 2026.
- "The Colorado AI Act takes effect 30 June 2026" or "1 February 2026." Superseded; S.B. 189 replaces it, effective 1 Jan 2027 conditional on AG rules, and the discrimination duty and impact assessments were dropped.
- "SR 11-7 governs bank model risk." Superseded 17 April 2026 by SR 26-2, which also excludes generative and agentic AI.
- "Federal law preempts state AI laws" or "a federal moratorium is in force." No statute; EO 14365 directs litigation and funding conditions only; the 10-year moratorium was removed 99-1 in 2025 (search summary).
- "AIDA / Bill C-27 is pending." It lapsed January 2025 (search summary).
- "METR says AI task length doubles every 7 months" as the current rate. 7 months is the 2019-2025 average; METR's post-2023 fit is about 131 days and post-2024 about 89 days. Also do not present METR figures as general capability: tasks are mainly software, ML and cybersecurity, and over 16 hours is unreliable.
- "A 17.4-hour (or 3-hour) time horizon for Claude Mythos" as a METR figure. Only secondary sources; METR's wording is "at least 16 hours" with caveats; conflicting figures circulate.
- "280x cheaper inference" as a 2026 AI Index figure. It is from the 2025 edition (Nov 2022 to Oct 2024, GPT-3.5-level).
- "25 states have adopted the NAIC bulletin." Older; NAIC's 8 Oct 2026 map lists 29 jurisdictions including DC and Puerto Rico.
- "OECD tracks 1,000+ national AI policies." OECD's own wording is "over 800" from 69 countries and the EU.
- "Gartner says 40% of agentic projects will fail" as fact. It is a June 2025 prediction about cancellation by end of 2027.
- Any 2024 state-bill count. Sources differ by about a factor of two.
- "The UK has an AI Act" or "the UK will legislate in 2026." No bill in the 13 May 2026 King's Speech.
- Stanford 2026 legislative-mention counts. Not found; do not extrapolate from 2025.
- China labeling rules "issued 7 March 2025." Most sources say 14 March; use "effective 1 September 2025" only.
